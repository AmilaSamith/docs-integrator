Integration artifacts are the building blocks of every integration. WSO2 Agent Builder focuses on the AI-powered artifact types: chat agents, MCP services, RAG pipelines, and direct LLM calls. Choosing the right artifact for the job keeps your integration logic focused and your project easy to navigate.
